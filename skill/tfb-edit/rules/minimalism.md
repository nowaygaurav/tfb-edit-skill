# minimalism.md: less is more (his rule, 2026-11-12, every edit, any client)

**The human test (2026-11-12):** before adding any text ask "would a normal person say it like this?" and "will someone reading it get the context?" If either is no, rewrite or cut.

**The test, before adding anything to the screen:** "Would the video still make sense without this?" If yes, cut it. Optimise for the fewest things on the viewer's screen.

- No source lines, keys/legends ("1 dot = 1 year"), footnotes, watermarks or labels the viewer doesn't need. The voice already carries context.
- A label stays only if the frame stops making sense without it (e.g. "Start at birth" vs "Start 10 years later" on a comparison).
- Emphasis comes from motion and fading what's done (grey out the old number, hollow the lost dots), not from adding a new element.
- One idea per page. If a second idea needs space, it's a new beat.

**Why:** 2026-11-12, TFB "10 years" motion test: he said the source line bottom left "is not looking nice, the thing is looking off", and "our whole decision making is based on minimalism".
