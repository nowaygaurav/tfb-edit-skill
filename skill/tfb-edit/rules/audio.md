# audio.md: sound rules for TFB reels (locked 2026-11-12)

## 0b. MUTE-SAFE (LOCKED 2026-11-12, every video, any client)
**No beat may depend on sound to make sense.** Most viewers scroll on mute (PLOS ONE 2024 sound-off eye-tracking study): they lose every SFX, the music and his tone. So the picture + on-screen text must carry every beat alone (mute test, hierarchy.md step 1).
**Rule 1 stays:** every motion still gets a sound (felt, not heard). Sound adds feel for listeners; it never carries meaning.

## 0c. EXPLAINER / CLIENT MOTION SOUND (his rule 2026-11-12, first used on TFB explainer)
- **Voice = information. SFX = feeling.** A sound exists only to make the viewer *feel* what is being said (money landing, a jar filling, something missing). If the visual + voice already make them feel it, use silence.
- **No whooshes or swooshes.** Movement alone never gets a sound here.
- **Money moments get money sounds** (coin, clink, soft pour), and **few of them**: never a sound on every coin.
- **Never stack SFX.** One sound at a time; nothing that makes the mix contrasty or choppy.


## Levels (felt, not heard; peaks under the voice peak)
- soft coin / small coin: about -31 to -33 dB below 0 dBFS when the voice peaks near -2 dBFS
- bigger coin into jar: about -25 dBFS
- pour bed: about -31 dBFS, faded in and out
