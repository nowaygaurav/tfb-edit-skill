## The motion grammar they share
1. **Entrances are physical.** Scale pop 0 → 105% → 100% in 0.15–0.3 s, drops that land with a bounce, slides that decelerate hard. Nothing fades in flat.
2. **Objects carry the idea** (cash cube, house, book, phone, plane, jar), not abstract shapes. One object per idea.
3. **One object becomes the next.** A trail, a drop or a zoom leads into the next scene; hard cuts only on the beat.
4. **Nothing is ever fully still.** Gentle float, bob, slow push or parallax on whatever is holding.
5. **Numbers move with the voice.** They tick or pop exactly when said, sitting on the object they belong to.
6. **Colour = meaning.** One base (cream, black, or one bold flat colour) + one accent; green gain, red/grey loss.
7. **Few words, big.** Word-by-word pops, one keyword in the accent.
8. **Pace:** something changes every 0.5–1 s; a key stat holds 1.5–3 s.

## What this means for our TFB explainer (v4 → v5)
- Coins should physically drop *into the jar* and the level rise in steps with a little slosh/bounce, not a flat linear fill.
- Jars and calendar float gently while holding; the age counter pops on each tick.
- The year (calendar) should fly along a curved trail into the jar (p4 plane-trail logic).
- Lost years: the late jar's empty years shown as coins that never arrive (grey ghost coins), gain = green, loss = grey.
- Keep the TFB base from STYLE.md (off-white glow, dotted grid, deep green); borrow the sticker tilt only for small labels.
